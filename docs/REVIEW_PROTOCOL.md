# Independent review protocols

## Formative usability exercise (15 minutes per adult volunteer)
Use synthetic scenarios only. Explain that the prototype has no validated medical interpretation. Ask the participant to think aloud, without coaching until they finish or abandon a task.

1. Identify the next check and explain why it is needed.
2. Enter synthetic shift context and explain what changed.
3. Disable the workstation, then the tablet. Explain whether the review is complete.
4. Find measurement limitations and the next human-review step.
5. Export the packet. Explain whether someone has received it automatically.

Record participant code (no name needed), date, relevant experience, task completion, assistance, mistakes, exact confusion and whether they distinguish evidence completeness from health clearance. Note screen/device and prototype version. Do not induce fatigue or collect personal health details. Report actual sample size and all failures. This is formative convenience-sample feedback, not clinical validation.

- How should missed cues, early responses, input latency and device differences affect whether a task can support a review?
- Does the next-step text overinterpret any measurement or imply an unsafe omission?
- What review procedure and escalation content would require mission-specific approval?
