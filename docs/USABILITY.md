## Interpretation

The aggregate completion rate differs by 28 percentage points between rounds. The follow-up includes nine participants with prior exposure and only six new participants. Those cohorts cannot be treated as a controlled before/after comparison or used to isolate a causal redesign effect. Participant-level paired results were not supplied, so the returning group's change cannot be calculated.

The results document formative testing and iteration. They do not establish astronaut usability, clinical validity, medical clearance, reduced crew workload or an optimisation advantage over greedy. No clinical information or participant identifiers are published here.

## Suggested submission wording

We evaluated the equipment-loss scenario through two rounds of usability testing. Initially, 13 of 25 participants completed the scenario (52%). Observed difficulties informed revisions, which were subsequently tested by nine returning participants and six new participants. Twelve of these 15 participants completed the revised scenario (80%): eight returning participants and four new participants. The follow-up groups were small, and returning participants' familiarity limits conclusions about the size of the redesign's effect.
